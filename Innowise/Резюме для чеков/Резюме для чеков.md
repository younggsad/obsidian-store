# About ME

#### Self-intro

To save time, I'll tell you a little about myself. I'm a PHP developer, and for several years now I've been focusing on Drupal development. I've also worked with related technologies such as React and Next.js, along with the APIs and tools around them. I'm comfortable with JavaScript and TypeScript as well. My main domains are project management, workflow automation, analytics, and AI. I have also built applications in the areas of data visualization and real-time polling.

As for projects, I've been lucky to work in different teams and I've also worked as a key developer. On projects I've taken on different roles, from a regular developer to an acting tech lead while the lead was on vacation. It all depended on the client's needs. Since clients trusted me, I was able to try different roles and gain valuable experience. That's it in a nutshell. If you're interested in anything specific, please ask. #### Education

I studied computer science at the University of Warsaw (UW), at the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics (MIMUW). I started at 19 and graduated at 22 with a bachelor's degree (licencjat). I chose UW because it has one of the strongest computer science programs in Poland, with a solid focus on mathematics and algorithms.

The first year was the hardest. There was a lot of math, such as algebra and discrete mathematics, along with programming and algorithms. But that foundation helped me a lot later, because I learned how to break complex problems into smaller parts and think logically. In the later years, the courses became more practical, with databases, operating systems, networks and team projects.

#### My path into IT

My path into IT started with C++. I liked how logical programming is and how you can build something real from nothing. After that, I moved into frontend: I worked with React, JavaScript and TypeScript, and I really enjoyed seeing the results of my work right away in the browser. Then I became interested in what happens behind the scenes, so I started working on the backend too. Later I decided to try PHP, and it turned out to be a great choice. I discovered Drupal, and I've been working with it for several years now. I think this mix of backgrounds helps me see the full picture, from the interface to the server.

#### Career

I'm 25 now, and I've been working in IT for 5 years. I started working while I was still studying, so I gained real experience at the same time as I was getting my degree. It wasn't always easy to balance work and university, but it paid off: when I graduated, I already had about two years of professional experience and didn't have to start from scratch.

#### Warsaw

I live in Warsaw, in the Praga-Północ district, on the right bank of the Vistula. I chose it because it's a lively area with good parks and a nice mix of old and new buildings, and it's more affordable than the city center. My office is at Rondo Daszyńskiego 2B, in Wola.

My commute is easy. I walk about 7 minutes to the Dworzec Wileński metro station, then take the M2 line straight to Rondo Daszyńskiego. It's 6 stops and takes about 15 minutes, and the office is right next to the station. In total, it takes 25 to 30 minutes door to door. The best part is that I don't need to change lines, so even in winter it's a very comfortable ride.

# Full self-intro

Hello, my name is Evgeniy. I am a Software Engineer with 5 years of hands-on experience across both the backend and frontend of web applications. I started working during my studies at the University of Warsaw, where I earned a bachelor's degree in computer science. My main domains are project management, workflow automation, analytics, AI, and real-time polling with data visualization.

My main focus on the backend is PHP and Drupal, which I have been working with for several years. In Drupal, I develop custom modules, entities and services, working with the Plugin, Queue and Batch APIs, and I use Workflows and Content Moderation to enforce business rules on the server. Drupal is built on Symfony components, so I'm comfortable with dependency injection, event subscribers and modern PHP standards. For headless architectures and third-party integrations, I build REST and JSON:API endpoints, handling authentication and security across services.

Besides PHP, I have strong experience with TypeScript and JavaScript: React and Next.js on the frontend, and Node.js with Express on the backend. Thanks to this mix, I can take a feature from the database schema to the user interface.

On the data and infrastructure side, I work with MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL (through Prisma), SQL Server and Redis for caching. My DevOps toolkit includes Docker, Nginx, GitHub Actions and AWS: I have deployed applications to ECS Fargate with RDS, S3 and CloudFront, and I have written the Dockerfiles and CI/CD pipelines myself.

I pay close attention to code quality and automated testing, relying on PHPUnit, PHPStan, PHPCS, Playwright and Vitest. In my daily workflow, I also actively use AI coding assistants like Claude Code and Codex to boost productivity, and I always verify the results with tests and static analysis.

Across my projects, I design system architecture, implement new features, fix complex bugs, and set up security and CI pipelines. I have worked in teams of different sizes and on one project I acted as a tech lead while the lead was on vacation.

As a developer, I am passionate about system design, clean architecture and continuous learning. As a teammate, I am responsible, communicative and quick to onboard into new projects.
